About

I'm a Computer Science student at Toronto Metropolitan University, with a Co-op option (transferred from Carleton University). I'm also an OUA varsity athlete, a native Bulgarian speaker, and professionally proficient in French. I like working across the full process — from UX research and Figma prototypes to shipping the React and TypeScript that makes them real.

Alongside school, I co-founded D&M Detailing, a Toronto-based detailing startup, and work as a production assistant at Bensimon Byrne. I recently placed 2nd at the SESA Mini Hackathon, hosted by uOttawa. I design in Figma and build with React, Node.js, and Tailwind CSS.
